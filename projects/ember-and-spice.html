Our Philosophy

Rooted in Fire,
Elevated by Spice

Executive Chef Antoine Dubois approaches the hearth not as mere heat, but as a dynamic, living instrument. Each species of cured wood—from seasoned mountain hickory to sweet applewood charcoal—imparts a distinct aromatic note into pasture-raised proteins and heirloom botanicals.

We partner with biodynamic foragers across four continents to source wild sumac, fermented Tellicherry peppercorn, and smoked Spanish saffron, bridging ancestral woodcraft with avant-garde culinary restraint.
